About Me

Building Secure Digital Infrastructure

I'm a results-driven Senior Technical Officer with over 8 years of progressive experience in IT support, system administration, and cybersecurity. My journey began as a Customer Support Technician at E-W Information Systems Ltd, where I built strong foundations in OS management, networking, and hardware repair.

Throughout my career I've developed proven expertise in diagnosing and resolving complex hardware/software issues, managing enterprise networks, and implementing secure IT solutions. I'm passionate about cybersecurity and continuously work on strengthening data protection measures while ensuring compliance with industry standards.

Currently I lead IT operations at E-W Information Systems Ltd, supervising technical teams, overseeing critical infrastructure projects, and delivering IT solutions aligned with business objectives. I hold a BSc (Hons) in Computer Networks & Security and multiple industry certifications from Microsoft and Cisco.
